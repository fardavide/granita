Granita

Support

Granita connects your iPhone or iPad to the repositories you choose to share from your Mac.

Find and pair your Mac

If your Mac does not appear, check that Granita is running on the Mac, both devices are on the same local network, and Granita has local network access on your iPhone or iPad. For a new pairing code, open Granita in your Mac’s menu bar and choose Pair a device….

Report a problem

For a bug report or feature request, use Granita on GitHub. Include the Granita version, your device and OS version, and what you did before the problem. Keep repository contents, pairing codes, and diagnostic logs out of public reports.
