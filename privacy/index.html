Granita

Privacy Policy

Granita lets you read changes from Git repositories you enable on your Mac. A paired iPhone or iPad receives those changes directly from that Mac over an encrypted connection.

Pairing and review data

Granita has no account. Pairing information is stored in the device’s Keychain. Granita saves review comments on the device that created them and syncs them to your paired Mac when it is reachable. Reader preferences are stored on the device where you set them. When you copy a review, Granita places it on the system clipboard so you can paste it where you choose.

Permissions and deleting data

Granita uses local network access to find and connect to your Mac. It uses the camera when you choose to scan a pairing QR code. You can change those permissions in your device’s Settings. To clear the review on your iPhone or iPad and sync the change to the Mac, clear the review while the Mac is reachable. To stop sharing a repository or disconnect a device, turn off the repository or revoke the device in the Mac app.

Sharing and support

Granita does not send repository contents, comments, or app usage analytics to the developer. If you choose to share a review or send diagnostic information to support, the recipient receives the information you share.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. Read GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

For general privacy questions, see Granita Support.
